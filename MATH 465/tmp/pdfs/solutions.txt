1. A palindrome is determined completely by its first half, including the middle character when 𝑛 is odd. There are
   ⌈𝑛/2⌉ positions in this first half, and each may independently contain any of the three symbols. The remaining
   positions are then forced by symmetry. Therefore the number of palindromes is

                                                          3 ⌈𝑛/2⌉ .

2. First fix one person at a reference seat, which removes the rotational symmetry. The other six people can then be
   placed in 6! orders. Reversing the order around the table gives every person exactly the same two neighbors, so
   each seating has been counted twice. (With distinct people, an ordering cannot equal its own reversal.) Hence
   the number of different seatings is
                                                        6!
                                                           = 360 .

3. Let 𝑃 denote Pride and Prejudice and let 𝐸 denote Emma.

    (a) Treat 𝑃 and 𝐸 as a single block. The block and the other six books form seven objects, which can be arranged
        in 7! ways. The two books inside the block can appear as 𝑃𝐸 or 𝐸 𝑃, so the answer is

                                                       2 · 7! = 10,080 .

   (b) There are 8! arrangements in total. Subtracting the arrangements counted in part (a) gives

                                                     8! − 2 · 7! = 30,240 .

    (c) Swapping 𝑃 and 𝐸 defines a bijection between arrangements in which 𝑃 is to the left of 𝐸 and arrangements
        in which 𝐸 is to the left of 𝑃. These two cases divide all 8! arrangements equally. Thus the answer is

                                                         8!
                                                            = 20,160 .

4. The first digit has 9 choices, since it cannot be zero. After it is chosen, the second digit has 9 choices (zero is
   now allowed, but the first digit cannot be repeated). The remaining four positions have respectively 8, 7, 6, and 5
   choices. By the multiplication principle, the number is

                                              9 · 9 · 8 · 7 · 6 · 5 = 136,080 .
